About

Named after a ditch gate. Run like one.

A headgate is the gate at the top of an irrigation ditch — the thing that decides where the water goes. Marisol Vega grew up opening and closing them on her grandfather's farm near Brighton. She started Headgate in 2009 with one truck and a sewer camera.

Still local

Locally owned. Not private equity.

A lot of the Front Range's best-known plumbing brands now belong to national investment groups. There's nothing wrong with that — but it changes who the company answers to. Headgate is owned by the master plumber whose license it runs under, and she still takes the Monday dispatch meeting.

  • Say the price out loud.

    Published ranges online, a written price on site, and no work until you say yes.

  • Pay plumbers to fix things, not sell things.

    Our plumbers are paid hourly. Nobody here earns a bonus for a water heater.

  • Answer the phone.

    A person on the emergency line all night, who can walk you to your shutoff before a truck arrives.

  • Leave it better documented.

    Photos, camera video, and a written cause on every job, so the next plumber — or buyer — knows what's there.
