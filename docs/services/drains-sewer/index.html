Drains & sewer

One slow sink is a clog. Every drain gurgling at once is your main line — and in older Denver neighborhoods, that usually means roots in clay pipe.

Book an arrival window (303) 555-0142
Signs you need us
  • Water comes up in the basement floor drain when the washer drains
  • Toilets gurgle or bubble when a sink runs
  • More than one fixture is slow at the same time
  • A sewer smell outside near the cleanout
Not sure? Try the triage tool

How we handle it

  1. Step 1

    Clear it

    Cable from the fixture or cleanout for everyday clogs. Hydro-jetting when grease or roots keep coming back.

  2. Step 2

    See it

    Every main line clearing ends with a camera pass. You get the video and a footage map showing where the problem is and how deep.

  3. Step 3

    Fix it

    Spot repairs where one joint has failed. Trenchless lining when the whole lateral is tired and the yard, driveway or tree is worth saving.

Colorado note

In Denver, the sewer line is yours to the street

The property owner is responsible for the sewer lateral all the way to the city main — including the part under the sidewalk and street. Homes built before the 1970s usually have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg pipe, and our expansive clay soils shift joints enough to let roots in.

What's under your yard

Your sewer pipe depends on when the house was built.

BuiltUsual sewer pipeWhat to watch for
Before 1950Clay tile or cast ironJoints every 2–3 feet. Roots find them.
1945–1972Orangeburg (tar paper) in some homesDeforms and collapses with age. Replace, don't clear.
1950s–1970sClay or cast ironCast iron scales and cracks from the inside.
1975 and laterPVC or ABS plasticLong runs, few joints. Sags from shifting soil are the usual issue.

General guide — a camera inspection is the only way to know what's really there.

Straight answers

Should I pour chemical drain cleaner in first?
Please don't. It rarely clears a main line, it can damage older pipe, and it's dangerous for whoever opens the line next — including you.
Do I need a camera inspection before buying a house?
In any Denver home built before about 1980, yes. It's a few hundred dollars against a repair that can run five figures, and it's leverage in the negotiation.
Will you dig up my whole yard?
Usually not. Most laterals can be lined or burst from one or two small access pits.
