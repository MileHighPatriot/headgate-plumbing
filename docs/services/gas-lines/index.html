Services · Gas lines

Gas lines

If you smell gas right now, leave the house and call Xcel Energy first. Once the utility has made it safe, we find and fix the leak and pressure-test the system before anything relights.

Signs you need us
  • A rotten-egg smell near an appliance or meter
  • A hissing sound near a gas line
  • A new range, dryer or grill that needs a line
  • A failed gas test during a home sale
Not sure? Try the triage tool

How we handle it

  1. Step 1

    Leak repair

    Fitting-by-fitting leak checks, repairs and a full pressure test, with the results written up for your records or the buyer's inspector.

  2. Step 2

    New lines

    Black iron or CSST sized to the full load of the house, not just the new appliance. Permitted and inspected.

  3. Step 3

    Outdoor

    Grills, fire pits and patio heaters. Trenched, bonded and pressure-tested.

Colorado note

Xcel first, then us

Xcel Energy's gas emergency line is the right first call for any gas smell. They respond around the clock at no charge. Call them from outside, away from the house. We handle everything on your side of the meter after that.
