Price book

What things usually cost.

Typical ranges for the jobs we do most across the Denver metro. The big swings come from access — a crawlspace, a finished ceiling, a sewer under the driveway — and we'll tell you which end you're on before we start.

  • The range is the range.

    Your exact price is confirmed on site, in writing, before any work starts. No surprise line items.

  • $89 to come out.

    Credited to the repair if you go ahead. Waived for Care Plan members.

  • Nights and Sundays cost more.

    A flat $149 after-hours fee, told to you on the phone before we roll.

  • Permits are included.

    When a job needs a permit — water heaters, gas lines, sewer repairs — it's in the price.
