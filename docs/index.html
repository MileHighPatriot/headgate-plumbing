How your plumbing works

Follow the water through the house.

  1. Stop 1 of 5It starts under the streetOne buried service line carries water from the main to your house. When it leaks, the first sign is often a wet patch in the yard or a bill that jumps.Water lines & repiping
  2. Stop 2 of 5The valve that stops everythingThe main shutoff sits where the line comes through the wall. Knowing where yours is turns a burst pipe from a flood into a puddle.Whose pipe is it?
  3. Stop 3 of 5Then it gets heatedA tank heater works every hour of every day. Most give some warning before they quit: rusty water, rumbling, or a slow drip at the base.Water heaters
  4. Stop 4 of 5Up to every tapHot and cold lines branch to each sink, toilet and tub. A running toilet or a dripping faucet is small, steady money down the drain.Leaks & fixtures
  5. Stop 5 of 5And out through one drainEvery drain in the house joins a single line back to the street. If they all gurgle at once, that line is where the trouble is.Drains & sewer
